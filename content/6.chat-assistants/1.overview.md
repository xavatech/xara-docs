A chat assistant is a contact customers find in the Xara app's assistant directory and chat with like a person. Every message they send goes to your **webhook**. Your server replies through the [assistant API](/chat-assistants/api) with the same API token you use for the rest of Xara. You write the bot; Xara carries the chat, the customer's identity, and payments.

## How an assistant works

1. A customer finds your assistant in **Chat → Assistants** and opens a chat with it.
2. They send a message: text, a photo, or a voice note. Xara saves it and POSTs an `assistant.message` event to your webhook, signed with the assistant's secret. See [Webhook events](/chat-assistants/webhooks).
3. Your server replies with `POST /ChatAssistant/reply`, quoting the message if you like. You can show a typing indicator first with `ChatAssistant/typing`.
4. To charge the customer, send a payment request with `ChatAssistant/requestPayment`. They pay it from their wallet in the chat, and Xara sends you `assistant.payment` with the request's `request_id`. The money goes to your business.

You can only reply to customers who have messaged the assistant first. Customers can't send stickers, videos, or contact cards to an assistant.

## Submit an assistant

1. Host a webhook over **https** that accepts JSON POSTs and answers with a `2xx` status.
2. In the [business dashboard](https://business.usexara.ai/dashboard/chat-assistants), open **Chat Assistants** and select **Submit an assistant**. Only the business owner can.
3. Enter a name, a short description customers see in the directory, and your webhook URL. Optionally add **notes for your bot**: Xara sends them with every event as `assistant.branding`, and customers never see them.
4. Optionally upload a logo: a PNG, JPEG, or WebP under 2 MB. Without one, customers see your business logo.

Once saved, the card shows the assistant's **ref** (the id you pass to every API call) and its **webhook secret**. Select **Test webhook** to send a signed `assistant.ping` and check your server answers. A business can have up to 5 assistants.

## Review

Our team reviews every assistant. We check that the name and logo don't impersonate another brand, the description says what it does, and the webhook answers. Once approved, the assistant appears in the directory and its status in the dashboard reads **Live**. If it is rejected, the dashboard shows why; fix it and save to send it for review again.

::warning
Changing an approved assistant's name sends it back for review, and customers can't find or message it until it's approved again. Changing its description, logo, notes, or webhook URL doesn't.
::

## Turn an assistant off

Select **Turn off** on the assistant's card to stop it without losing its customers. While it's off, customers can't message it, Xara sends no events, and the API refuses replies. Select **Turn on** to bring it back; it doesn't need another review.

## Keep the secret safe

The webhook secret proves events come from Xara. Keep it on your server, next to your API token. If it leaks, select **Replace** next to the secret: Xara signs every event after that with the new one, so update your server straight away.
