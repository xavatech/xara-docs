This quickstart takes an existing product ID and requests an invoice for a customer. The request and response are shown beside the guide.

## Before you begin

Get your **API token** from the [Xara developer dashboard](https://business.usexara.ai/dashboard/developers). You will also need the ID of a product that belongs to the authenticated business.

If you do not have a product yet, [create one first](/api-reference/products).

## 1. Build the invoice items

Every invoice needs at least one `product_id` and its `quantity`.

Send `items` as a normal JSON array:

```json
"items": [{ "product_id": 128, "quantity": 1 }]
```

Each `product_id` must belong to the business associated with your API token.

## 2. Request the invoice

Send `customerPhoneNumber` and `items` to `Payment/initiateInvoice`. Include your API token in the `Authorization` header using the Bearer scheme.

The example on the right can be switched between cURL and JavaScript.

## 3. Store the reference

Keep the returned `reference` with your order:

```text
INV-…
```

You will use it to match the payment webhook to the correct customer or order.

## 4. Handle payment

Register a webhook URL in your business dashboard, then follow the [payment webhook guide](/webhooks/payment-webhooks) to verify the request and record the payment.
